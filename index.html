Welcome to Arc Raiders Info

Arc Raiders is an upcoming cooperative third-person shooter set in a reimagined Wasteland. Work together with your squad to survive against the mysterious ARC machines and emerge victorious.

About Arc Raiders

Set in a post-apocalyptic world, Arc Raiders challenges players to band together and face off against an alien threat known as the ARC.
Form squads, scavenge for resources, and strategize your approach to overcome increasingly difficult challenges. Every raid is different, and every decision matters.

Key Features

Cooperative Gameplay

Team up with friends in squads of up to 4 players. Coordination and communication are key to survival.

Advanced Arsenal

Discover and customize a wide variety of weapons and equipment to match your playstyle.

Dynamic World

Explore an ever-changing wasteland with unpredictable events and environmental challenges.

Gameplay

Experience heart-pounding action as you navigate through dangerous territories filled with ARC machines. Each encounter requires tactical thinking and quick reflexes.
Adapt your strategy on the fly, use the environment to your advantage, and work with your team to overcome seemingly impossible odds.

  • PvE focused cooperative gameplay
  • Resource management and crafting
  • Progressive difficulty system
  • Unique character abilities
